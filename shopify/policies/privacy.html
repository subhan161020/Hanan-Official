What we collect and why

Name, email, delivery address, order history and, if you consent, marketing preferences. We use this to fulfil your order, handle returns, and — only with your consent — send you emails. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep your bag working. Analytics and advertising cookies are only set if you accept them in the banner. You can change your mind at any time.
