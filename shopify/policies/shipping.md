# Shipping policy

## UK delivery

Standard (Royal Mail Tracked 48) — £3.95, free on orders over £120.
Usually 2–3 working days.

Express (Royal Mail Tracked 24) — £5.95. Next working day if ordered
before 2pm.

We dispatch Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays.

## International delivery

Calculated at checkout by weight and destination. Import duties and local
taxes are payable by you on arrival unless the checkout states otherwise.

---

**Before publishing:** these rates must match the shipping rates configured
under Settings → Shipping and delivery, and the free-over-£120 threshold
must exist there as a rate condition — not only as a promise in this text
and the announcement bar. Same-day dispatch before 2pm is a promise you
have to be able to keep every working day.
