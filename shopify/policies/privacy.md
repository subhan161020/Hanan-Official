# Privacy policy

## What we collect and why

Name, email, delivery address, order history and, if you consent, marketing
preferences. We use this to fulfil your order, handle returns, and — only
with your consent — send you emails. Card details are handled by our payment
provider and never reach us.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time.
We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

## Cookies

Essential cookies keep your bag working. Analytics and advertising cookies
are only set if you accept them in the banner. You can change your mind at
any time.

---

**Before publishing, add:**

- Your ICO registration number
- Your data retention periods
- Your processors. On Shopify this list changes from the original site:
  Shopify itself (hosting, checkout, customer accounts), your payment
  provider, Royal Mail, and any email or analytics apps you install.
  Cloudflare comes off the list once the site no longer runs there.
- Your cookie categories, and a consent banner that actually gates the
  non-essential ones. Shopify has a built-in cookie banner under
  Settings → Customer privacy.
