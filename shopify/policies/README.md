# Policy drafts

These are the policy texts from the original site, split to match the slots
Shopify gives you under **Settings → Policies**. Paste each one into its slot.

Shopify links these from the checkout and from the footer, and is bound by
what they say — so they live there, not in the theme. The theme's policies
page reads whatever is published here, which keeps one copy rather than two
that can drift apart.

| File | Shopify slot |
|---|---|
| `shipping.md` | Shipping policy |
| `refund.md` | Refund policy |
| `privacy.md` | Privacy policy |
| `terms.md` | Terms of service |

**These are drafts written to reflect UK consumer law, not legal advice.**
Have them checked before trading — the wording carries legal weight, and the
gaps marked `[ ]` have to be filled in first.
