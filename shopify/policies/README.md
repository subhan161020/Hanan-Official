# Policy drafts

These are the policy texts from the original site, split to match the slots
Shopify gives you under **Settings → Policies**. Paste each one into its slot.

Shopify links these from the checkout and from the footer, and is bound by
what they say — so they live there, not in the theme. The theme's policies
page reads whatever is published here, which keeps one copy rather than two
that can drift apart.

| File | Shopify slot | Paste-ready |
|---|---|---|
| `shipping.md` | Shipping policy | `shipping.html` |
| `refund.md` | Refund policy | `refund.html` |
| `privacy.md` | Privacy policy | `privacy.html` |
| `terms.md` | Terms of service | — see below |

The `.html` files are the policy text alone, ready for the `<>` view in
Shopify's policy editor. The `.md` versions are the same text plus the
notes on what to fill in — **the notes are for you, not for customers**, so
paste from the HTML.

**There is no `terms.html`, deliberately.** `terms.md` is a list of what
terms of service must cover, not terms themselves. Start from Shopify's
template at Settings → Policies → Terms of service → "Create from template"
and check it against that list.

**These are drafts written to reflect UK consumer law, not legal advice.**
Have them checked before trading — the wording carries legal weight, and the
gaps marked `[ ]` have to be filled in first.
