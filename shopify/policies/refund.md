# Refund policy

## Your legal right to cancel

You have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it,
without giving a reason. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods
back. We refund the full price plus our standard delivery charge within 14
days of receiving the goods, or of you proving you have sent them.

To cancel, email us at [your email address] with your order number, or use
the cancellation form included in your parcel.

> The 14-day cancellation right is the legal minimum. If this information is
> missing or wrong, the cancellation period extends to 12 months. Do not
> remove this section.

## Our 30-day returns policy

We go beyond the legal minimum: return anything unworn with tags attached
within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Return postage for change of
mind is paid by you. We do not charge a restocking fee.

## Faulty or wrong items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you can reject faulty goods within 30
days for a full refund. Contact us and we will cover return postage and put
it right.
