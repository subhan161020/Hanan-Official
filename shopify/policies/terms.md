# Terms of service

The original site did not carry terms of service — this slot was never
drafted, and Shopify offers a template you can start from under
Settings → Policies → Terms of service → "Create from template".

Whatever you use has to state, at minimum:

- Your trading name, geographic address and contact email
- Your company number, if you trade as a limited company
- Your VAT number, if registered
- When a contract is formed (normally on dispatch, not on payment)
- Pricing and currency, and that taxes are included
- That sizes 52–58 are garment lengths in inches, not body sizes, so a
  customer cannot claim they were misled on fit

This is the same legal-identity information the site flags as missing in
several places — the contact page, the footer, and the About copy.
