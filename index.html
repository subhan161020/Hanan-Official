Our story

A word that had to become a coat

حَنان — hanan. Not love, exactly. The Arabic is more specific than that: the softness a mother turns towards her child. It is the sound in a voice, not a feeling in a chest.

The story behind Hanan

Born From A Need

HANAN began with a simple need I couldn't find on the market.

As a breastfeeding mother, I wanted an abaya that gave me full, effortless coverage while allowing me to breastfeed comfortably and discreetly.

I searched for something that felt genuinely modest, feminine and elegant — with nursing access that was practical, yet completely concealed within the design.

I couldn't find it.
So I decided to create it.

Designed With Intention

At the heart of every HANAN abaya is a concealed opening, secured with discreet press buttons or zip.

When closed, the opening becomes part of the abaya's cut. Nothing is visible, and nothing takes away from the silhouette.

When needed, it opens to provide comfortable nursing access.

Made For Every Woman

Although HANAN was born from the experience of breastfeeding, it was never intended to be only for breastfeeding mothers.

It is for any woman who appreciates an abaya that is beautifully cut, thoughtfully made and designed to last.

The nursing opening doesn't change the way the abaya looks or defines the piece. It simply gives you an option — something there when you need it, and seamlessly part of the design when you don't.

Our Philosophy

We believe practicality and elegance should never have to compete.

Modesty should not mean compromising comfort. Motherhood should not mean compromising personal style. And clothing designed for motherhood should still feel beautiful long after that chapter has changed.

HANAN is our answer to that belief:
Thoughtful design.
Timeless modesty.
Made with motherhood in mind.

Size chart

Abaya sizes are traditionally the garment length in inches, not a body size. Choose by your height first, then check the bust measurement.

Standard sizing

Measurements are taken flat and are approximate to within 1 in / 2.5 cm. Our abayas are cut loose — the bust figure is the garment, not your body.

How to measure

Length

Stand in the shoes you would normally wear. Measure from the top of your shoulder, beside your neck, straight down to where you want the hem to sit. Most women want it just off the floor.

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level. If you are nursing, measure while full rather than after a feed — that is the size you need it to work at.

Sleeve

From the shoulder point down the outside of a slightly bent arm to the wrist bone.

Between two sizes?

Go up. An abaya that is slightly long reads as elegant; one that is short does not. If you are between sizes on height but smaller on bust, the size chart on each product page will say whether that style runs generous.

Delivery, returns & privacy

These are drafts written to reflect UK consumer law. Have them checked before you trade — the wording carries legal weight.

We dispatch Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays.

International delivery

Calculated at checkout by weight and destination. Import duties and local taxes are payable by you on arrival unless the checkout states otherwise.

Returns and your cancellation rights

Your legal right to cancel

You have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. You then have a further 14 days to send the goods back. We refund the full price plus our standard delivery charge within 14 days of receiving the goods, or of you proving you have sent them.

To cancel, email us at Hanantmodesty@gmail.com with your order number, or use the cancellation form included in your parcel.

Note: the 14-day cancellation right is the legal minimum. If this information is missing or wrong, the cancellation period extends to 12 months. Do not remove this section.

Our 30-day returns policy

We go beyond the legal minimum: return anything unworn with tags attached within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Return postage for change of mind is paid by you. We do not charge a restocking fee.

Faulty or wrong items

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you can reject faulty goods within 30 days for a full refund. Contact us and we will cover return postage and put it right.

Privacy

What we collect and why

Name, email, delivery address, order history and, if you consent, marketing preferences. We use this to fulfil your order, handle returns, and — only with your consent — send you emails. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach us.

You can ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

To complete: add your ICO registration number, your data retention periods, your processors (e.g. Cloudflare, MailerLite, Zoho Mail, your payment provider, Royal Mail) and your cookie categories.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep your bag working. Analytics and advertising cookies are only set if you accept them in the banner. You can change your mind at any time.
